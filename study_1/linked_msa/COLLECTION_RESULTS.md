# Collection results

Training documentation updated November 10, 2026. The [current handoff](TRAINING_HANDOFF.md) specifies the next HQ feature comparison; no new training results are reported here.

The linked data structure is populated for all 50 metros and the fixed Fortune 500 roster. It contains 32,400 regional indicator cells, 500 inherited publisher locations, 253 additional company/locality groups and 834 role observations. The 254 new factual assertions cover 112 firms; one locality is supported by more than one assertion. Twenty-one broad company-industry groups appear in the coverage audit.

All 500 companies received an initial primary-source screening attempt. Indexed text was available for 460; a missing result is unresolved. The retained function facts cover headquarters/co-HQs, divisional management, research/development, engineering, internal finance/shared services, administrative work, corporate sales/customer services and unspecified offices. There are 336 new role observations because one location assertion can support several roles. These are neither 336 sites nor 336 distinct companies.

The original study is preserved: 498 eligible HQ firms, 435 selected HQ points inside the frame, and Atlanta's 15. The earlier fitted models and reported conclusions are unchanged. Block's distributed-HQ disclosure is separately flagged for the broader study rather than silently rewriting the old benchmark.

The collection provides concrete evidence that a primary-HQ count misses corporate work elsewhere. For example, CarMax's filing documents Atlanta finance-office and customer-experience activity despite its Richmond HQ assignment. Other filings identify additional divisional headquarters, research locations and administrative operations across industries. These observations improve what can be described; they do not establish an Atlanta deficit or explain why a company chose a location. Factual assertions and source routes are in raw `reviewed_office_facts.csv`.

The all-industry regional detail also makes corporate-management activity visible separately from HQ counts. Atlanta's CBP 2023 NAICS 55 records report **107,972 jobs and 1,125 establishments**, approximately **4.01%** of CBP employment. This is the broad management-of-companies sector, including businesses outside the Fortune roster. Atlanta's QCEW 2024 management-sector employment is suppressed and stays null. These two source years/programs are not interchangeable estimates of named-company office employment.

The major remaining limitation is office coverage. One locality has a retained explicit employment count and 23 have retained explicit office area. These sparse figures are observations from this review, not a claim that every published space/jobs figure was extracted. Eight additional locality groups lack an accepted MSA assignment. Public filings disclose properties unevenly: the industry audit shows substantial differences even under a uniform source-screening protocol. None of the 500 firms has a completed absence-capable national office audit. The office scorecard therefore reports evidence lower bounds and unknown coverage, without rankings or weights.

The next collection priority is a consistent directory/site-evidence review across industries, emphasizing the companies and functions with incomplete evidence under the same rules. Resolve ambiguous localities and dates, distinguish occupied offices from owned/planned/sublet space, and validate a common reporting period before comparing office gaps. Then review a limited set of improved predictors against the unchanged HQ benchmark. Parallel broader-outcome models remain a later, separately specified step.

The collection baseline passed 449 acceptance checks. Current validation also audits the explicitly recorded documentation revisions; see [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md) and the current [validation summary](reports/validation_summary.json). Checks cover keys, links, source/input hashes, missingness and suppression handling, the complete company–metro/year coverage grid, old predictor values and preservation of data/models. No new model training, weighted scoring or bespoke tech/healthcare extension was performed in this collection.
