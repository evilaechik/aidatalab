# City economic data

**All 50 metros in the [top-50 GDP list](top_50_us_metro_areas_by_gdp.md) are collected:** nine requested datasets per metro, **450 data files across 50 separate city folders**. Each folder also contains a README, source manifest and data dictionary. [Batch 1](collection_batch_01.md), [batch 2](collection_batch_02.md), [batch 3](collection_batch_03.md) and the [final batch of 11](collection_batch_04.md) added the 41 missing metros on October 27, 2026. Read [datasets_context.md](datasets_context.md) for the nine-file overview, matching year windows and interpretation limits, and [research context](../research_context.md) for the project design. Hartford and Bridgeport's 2023 planning-region GDP is unavailable in BEA and retained as `(NA)` source rows; only complete 2024 metro GDP totals are derived. Current Fortune headquarters verification is incomplete for the 48 metros added after Dallas/Denver; Seattle and Detroit have flagged publisher-location conflicts. Sacramento and Salt Lake City have zero matched 2025 Fortune map locations, documented in their header-only files. The batch summaries explain GDP reference-year differences, combined Virginia reporting areas and native-geography breaks. The [final report](collection_batch_04.md#complete-collection-validation) records the successful audit of all 450 saved files.
